# Volo — 10-Slide Pitch Deck Outline

Plan 11.2 Step 3. One screen per slide; speaker notes below each.

## Slide 1 — Title
**Volo — Financial Accountability for Screen Time**
"Put money where your attention is."

## Slide 2 — Problem
**$1B lost to the attention economy annually.**
We don't lack willpower — we lack stakes. Resolutions are free, so breaking
them is free.

## Slide 3 — Product
**Stake USDC → challenge a friend → stay off TikTok.**
Set a daily limit, lock a devnet stake, invite an opponent by `.skr` handle or
address. Hit your limit: keep your stake. Blow it: your opponent takes it.

## Slide 4 — Tech
**Android native + Solana devnet + MWA + Seed Vault.**
Kotlin/Compose UI, on-chain escrow program (Anchor), Mobile Wallet Adapter
signing, Supabase oracle evaluating usage reports, Room + 5 s account polling.

proof.

## Slide 7 — SKR angle
**10 SKR gates Open Duels; 100 SKR unlocks leaderboards.**
Gate is live in the Profile tab (balance read from the SKR mint on-chain).

## Slide 8 — Seeker integration

**150k Seeker users + a growing attention economy.**
