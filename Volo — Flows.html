THREE FLOWS.

Create, Lost and Onboarding, built only from the Volo design system. Create is one decision per screen. Onboarding explains the idea before it asks for anything. For Lost you get both options to compare.

CREATE

Seven screens: six decisions and the sent state. The opponent step offers recent opponents, paste, and an invite link, so nobody has to type a wallet address by hand.

LOST

A is calm and factual. B mirrors the win at full volume. The third screen is the both-failed Recovery lock, which both options share.

ONBOARDING

Story first, then each permission arrives with its reason. It uses the same progress bar as Create.
