# NOTE — Nopeek v3 (45s launch film)

## The one idea and why
Resolutions are free, so breaking them is free. The film performs that: a limit gets crossed, nothing happens — then money makes it matter. Chosen because it is the product truth (spec logline) and survives the cover test: with NOPEEK covered, the tape-duel-settle arc still only fits a stake-on-screen-time app.

## Beats and what each buys
- 0-7 Problem (RESOLUTIONS ARE FREE / SO IS BREAKING THEM + OVER gag that sits). Buys: the viewer feels the costlessness.
- 7-11 Turn (So put $5 on it → volt flood → whole phone arrives). Buys: the belief there is a fix.
- 11-16 Home (real HomeScreen + "This is a duel."). Buys: it is a real app, not a concept.
- 16-34 How (Pick/Limit/Stake/Rival/Lock, cursor-first, result ≥1s). Buys: I could do this.
- 34-40 Proof (live duel + "The phone counts." → breach coral → WON $10.00 + tx 5Hq2…tK9x + "The money settles itself."). Buys: the phone counts and the money moves itself.
- 40-45 CTA (eye-and-wall → NOPEEK → hairline home → Get Nopeek now. + footnote). Buys: what to do next.
